Acctax (Online) Ltd

Acctax (Online) Ltd is on HMRC's list of software recognised for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. HMRC lists it as bridging software that connects to records, a paid product, covering Sole trader, UK property, Foreign property quarterly updates and the end-of-year tax return.

Of the 16 other tax-return income items HMRC tracks, 13 are ready now, 3 in development.
