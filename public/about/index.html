About MTD Software Finder

MTD Software Finder is an independent, searchable copy of HMRC's list of software recognised for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, with one page per product and a nightly log of changes.

Disclaimer: a software list, not tax advice

We list software. We do not give tax, accounting or legal advice, and nothing here tells you whether you must use MTD, which product suits your affairs, or how to complete a return. For that, see GOV.UK, HMRC, or a qualified accountant. HMRC says: “All software listed has been through HMRC's recognition process. HMRC does not recommend any product or software provider.” Neither do we. Inclusion and order are not endorsements. Features and availability can change at any time — always confirm with the provider before you buy.

Where the data comes from

Product names, feature statuses, income sources, specifications and provider website links come from HMRC's Find software that works with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax service, re-read nightly. Deadlines and explanations quote GOV.UK pages, linked where used. Last checked: 29 October 2026. Pricing is not published by HMRC and we don't guess it.

How pages are built

Every list states the HMRC field it filters on. “Tax-return items ready” counts how many of the 16 other tax-return items HMRC marks Ready now for that product. We sort A–Z rather than ranking, because the data doesn't support a “best”.
